The Short Answer

Which is cheapest: Zapier, Make or n8n?

It depends on how long your workflows are. For a handful of short ones, Make is cheapest at $16 a month for 10,000 credits, and its free plan runs two. Once a workflow has several steps, n8n pulls ahead, because it bills one execution per run however many steps it has: $24 a month for 2,500 runs. Zapier costs the most per step, from $29.99 for 750 tasks, and it's worth it when you need an app only Zapier connects to.

We've built on all three. For something quick and simple we'll often use Make, and for anything bigger, including AI agents, we build on n8n.

Common Questions

Questions owners ask about these platforms

Is n8n free?

The software is free to run on your own server for your own business, including commercial use. n8n Cloud, where n8n hosts it for you, starts at $24 a month. What its license doesn't allow is reselling access to n8n or putting your own brand on it.

Is Make cheaper than Zapier?

Usually, and by a lot. Make's entry plan is $16 a month for 10,000 credits, and Zapier's is $29.99 for 750 tasks. Both charge for every step on every item, so a workflow that loops over a long list costs more on either one than it would on n8n.

Why is Zapier so expensive?

Zapier charges for each successful action, and its entry plan includes 750 of them. What you get for it is the biggest app library, 10,237 apps, and the easiest editor of the three. Triggers, filters, formatting and delay steps are free, so the real bill is lower than many comparisons claim.

What's the difference between a task, a credit and an execution?

A Zapier task is one successful action. A Make credit is one module run, and some AI modules cost more than one. An n8n execution is one full run of a workflow, however many steps it has, so five steps cost 5 tasks, 5 credits or 1 execution.

What happens if I go over my plan's limit?

Zapier holds the extra runs so you can replay them, or bills each task if you've turned that on, at 1.25 times the normal rate on annual plans and 2.5 times on monthly. Make stops your scenarios until you add credits. On n8n Cloud's Starter and Pro plans, new runs fail until the 1st of the month.

Can n8n run AI agents?

Yes. n8n has an AI Agent node that calls a model and can use tools and memory, and each agent run counts as one execution like any other workflow. You pay the model provider, such as Anthropic, directly for what the agent uses.

Should I self-host n8n or use n8n Cloud?

For most small businesses, n8n Cloud. A $12 to $24 server saves a few dollars a month, and then someone has to keep it updated and backed up. We only self-host when a client has to keep data on their own infrastructure, which is the case with patient records.

What's the best AI agent framework in 2026?

It depends on the team. CrewAI and LangGraph are the most used in Python, Mastra is the main TypeScript option, and the Claude Agent SDK suits agents that work with files and code. Most small businesses don't need one at all, because an agent built in n8n covers the common jobs.

Who owns the account when a consultant builds my automations?

You should. We open every account in your name, you pay the vendors directly, and we work in it as an added user. If we stop working together, you remove our login and everything keeps running.
