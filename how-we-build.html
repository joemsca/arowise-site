The Short Answer

Where do your automations run, and who owns them?

They run on n8n Cloud, a hosted service that works around the clock, and Claude does the reading and writing. Every account is opened in your name and you pay the vendors directly, from $24 a month plus AI usage as of October 2026. We work in those accounts as an added user, so if we ever part ways, you remove our login and everything keeps running.

The Architecture

How do the pieces connect?

n8n starts every workflow, on a schedule or the moment something happens, and Claude does the reading and writing. You see the results in your inbox or Slack, and every run is logged to a shared spreadsheet.

Why The Cloud

Why not run it on a computer in the office?

A laptop stops when the lid closes

A workflow that lives on someone's computer stops when it goes to sleep or leaves the building. A cloud account keeps running at 2am on a Sunday.

A rented server needs looking after

Running n8n on your own server saves about $12 a month, and then someone has to keep it updated and backed up. We only do it when you need to keep data in-house.

Patient records

If a workflow touches patient records, every service in the chain has to sign a business associate agreement, and n8n Cloud doesn't offer one. Those builds run on a setup that does and cost more, so we price them before we start.

Common Questions

Questions owners ask before we build

Where do my automations run?

On n8n Cloud, a hosted service that runs around the clock, with Claude doing the reading and writing. Nothing runs on a computer in your office, so nothing stops when someone closes a laptop.

What does it cost to run each month?

From $24 a month for n8n Cloud plus AI usage, as of October 2026. Drafting 1,000 email replies with Claude costs roughly $11 to $15. A database adds $25 a month, and only when a workflow has to remember things.

What happens if we stop working with AroWise Automation?

Nothing stops. Every account is in your name and you pay the vendors directly, so you remove our login and your workflows keep running.

Do I have to learn n8n?

No. You approve and edit work from your inbox or Slack, and every run is logged in a Google Sheet you can read. The n8n canvas is where we work.

Can it handle patient records?

Yes, on a different setup. Every service that touches patient records has to sign a business associate agreement, and n8n Cloud doesn't offer one, so those builds run on infrastructure that does. They cost more, and we price them before we start.
