/ 01What is AI Automation?

Systems that do your work for you, automatically.

Most people think of AI as a "more powerful Google" - you type a question, you get an answer out. You ask for an image, it makes one. Helpful, but you're still the one doing the work. We build affordable mini "AI employees" (AI Agents or workflows) that work day and night whether you're there or not.

For example: A lead comes in and gets a reply in under a minute, in your voice. Invoices go out instantly at project milestones. A customer from last year gets a quick check-in and orders again. All the to-do list items you wish you had time for, they just get done.

We don't automate human judgment, relationships, the mission-critical service that sets you apart. But almost everything else can (and should) be automated.

/ 04Business Process Framework

Refine the process, then automate it.

The goal is fantastic results for your business. We'll work with you to optimize your systems even before automating.

  1. C

    Challenge assumptions

    Decide what's truly required to get the desired results.

  2. R

    Remove what you can

    Delete steps, parts, & meetings wherever possible.

  3. I

    Improve what's left

    Optimize the remaining workflow, reduce costs.

  4. S

    Speed it up

    Fix bottlenecks to accelerate the whole process.

  5. P

    Program it

    Automate last. Build in error handling and retries.

This is why sometimes we'll tell you a process should be cut instead of automated.
