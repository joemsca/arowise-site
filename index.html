About

Who's behind it.

AroWise Automation is run by me, Joe Mascaro. I co-founded and ran a company that sold many millions of dollars of products to architecture, design, and construction clients, followed by a successful exit in 2026. Along the way I automated the repeatable work inside it: outreach, quoting, follow-ups, CRM hygiene, lead filtering, and reporting, which kept profits up while the team ran leaner.

I've seen firsthand where automation creates meaningful business value, and where it doesn't. I focus on systems that have a measurable impact on revenue, profitability, or operating efficiency. Before automating a process, I first look for ways to make it more efficient; automating a flawed process only compounds the problem. And while I believe much of the work around your business can be automated, the human element should not be. I build systems that support your people, and you'll have full access and ownership of every system I build for you.
