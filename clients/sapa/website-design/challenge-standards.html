Expert Pistol
Challenge Standards

Five drills, four levels. Run them after any SAPA training class to earn a spot on the list, and at Advanced or Expert, the SAPA challenge coin.

Expert Pistol Challenge Standards

Each drill has a time for every level. Shorter bar, faster time.

1) 3&2 Drill

At 3 yrds draw and engage 3 shots to the body 2 shots to the head

  1. Novice 3.25 sec
  2. Intermediate 2.50 sec
  3. Advanced 2.20 sec
  4. Expert 2.00 sec

2) 1R1

At 3 yrds high compressed position. Begin retreating, shoot 1 round, emergency reload, shoot 1 round (all center mass)

  1. Novice 5.00 sec
  2. Intermediate 3.50 sec
  3. Advanced 3.00 sec
  4. Expert 2.50 sec

3) Draw Stroke

From concealment at 7 yds, draw and fire 1 round. Run this drill two times and add both times together.

  1. Novice 4.00 sec
  2. Intermediate 3.00 sec
  3. Advanced 2.30 sec
  4. Expert 2.00 sec

4) Bill Drill

At 7 yrds draw and engage center mass with 6 shots

  1. Novice 4.00 sec
  2. Intermediate 3.00 sec
  3. Advanced 2.20 sec
  4. Expert 2.00 sec

5) Stoppage Drill with a Twist

At 15 yrds draw & engage 2 to the body 1 to the head, then sprint to 5 yrds line and engage 1 to the head 2 to the body

  1. Novice 12.00 sec
  2. Intermediate 9.00 sec
  3. Advanced 7.00 sec
  4. Expert 6.50 sec

Where the hits have to land

Novice

Novice: Center mass shots in the white area of body and head shots in the white area of the head.

Intermediate

Intermediate: Center mass shots within the 10" circle & within the white area of the head.

Advanced

Advanced: Center mass shots within the 10" circle & within the white area of the head.

Expert

Expert: Center mass shots within the 8" circle and within the 4.5" circle of the head.

Your level is the total

*NOTE: Times above are only a guideline. The actual times for each level is a sum of each drill added together.

  1. Novice total time: 40.00 seconds
  2. Intermediate total time: 25.00 seconds
  3. Advanced Total Time: 17.00 seconds
  4. Expert Total Time: 15.00 seconds

Line breakers only count if the carbon ring cuts the line. Ammunition must be factory ammunition, simulating the actual grain and recoil one would expect with self defense ammunition.

How this works

How this works: Target must be a SAPA target or IALEFI-Q target. Anyone who attends a training class with Small Arms Pistol Academy LLC will have the opportunity to run the Challenge Standards after the class. If you can meet all 5 with an Intermediate time your name will be added to the list online for bragging rights, it will only show the Top 10 most recent people. If you can meet all 5 drills within Adanced or Expert level you will earn an honorary SAPA challenge coin & your name will be added on the Advanced Pistol or Expert Pistol Challenge Completion list.

NOTE: Novice times essentially are industry standard times. Difficult for a brand new shooter to achieve but should be the goal for every gun owner to reach. Intermediate times are that those who shoot more should try to achieve. Advanced times are of high quality shooters, typically those who have attended additional training classes will strive to reach this level. Expert times are typically the type of shooting times expected when attending training classes with some world class organizations. Expert level is for the top 2% of firearm owners. Anyone is able to attempt this challenge; however, the only way to earn the coin or have your name added to the list, you MUST preform the standards at one of the Training Classes offered by SAPA.
