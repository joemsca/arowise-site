    Stippling, Engraving & Customization Services

    Stippling: Adding texture to your frame (stippling) is not only a great way to personalize your firearm, but its added benefits of increasing friciton of your grip gives shooters better control of their firearm.

    Engraving vs Etching: Engraving actually removes material while etching is a surface marking. Etching is displayed in the handcuff photo below while engravinig is displayed in the revolver below.

    Customization: With the use of a laser, CNC or other tools a wide variety such as optic cuts, porting, slide cuts, undercuts, memory cuts, or other services are possible.

    Small Arms Training Academy LLC holds a Type 7 FFL as a manufacture offering customization of your firearm. It is important to understand that modifying a firearm will void your factory warranty. However, humans add tattoos to their body to personalize you. So why not tattoo your firearm to personalize it? If you are interested in any type of service on your firearm, you can call or text 716-397-5769. My goal is have your gun completed and sent back to you ASAP. Because of this, you must ship your firearm or drop it off (if local) when it's your time for service.
