Marks on the exam form

A circle around a printed word, number or letter
that item applies (a level is involved, a view is ordered, a side, a test result)
Circle around P S after a ROM number
pain AND spasm on that motion; P alone = pain only; S alone = spasm only
A line or arrow from a row to a written word
that word belongs to that row (e.g. 'C/T pulling', 'restricted', 'reduces pain', 'avoids')
A brace } spanning several ROM rows with one phrase
the phrase applies to every row in the brace, said once at the end
'All C ROMs are WNL' circled
all cervical ranges of motion are within normal limits; no numbers
Small circled R or L above a circled level, joined by a line
the side of that fixation (C1 + circled R = C1 on the right)
One circle around two neighbouring numbers (e.g. 3,4)
both levels, same side, written as a range (C3-4)
In the 'L R SI' group: the letter(s) circled decide the side
L circled = left SI joint; L and R circled = both; whether SI is also circled changes nothing
A circle around SI alone, no letter
would mean bilateral sacroiliac joint (never seen yet)
+ circled in an orthopedic test row
positive; - circled = negative; L / R / Bi circled = side
A phrase written after an orthopedic test
what the test elicited (e.g. 'LBP central' = eliciting central low back pain)
A printed WNL crossed out
NOT within normal limits; the finding is written beside it or 'see below'
A printed WNL untouched
nothing is said about it in the note
A printed WNL with 'Pt states' / a tick / an arrow
within normal limits, as stated by the patient
A printed label crossed out and a new one written above it
the written label is the one that counts
'C5 view' circled (instead of 'Cervical')
cervical series INCLUDING flexion and extension views
'Cervical' / 'Thoracic' / 'Lumbar' circled
x-ray series taken today
'Tight hamstring muscles are found: Bilat'
should appear in the note as a sentence (it was missing from one typed note)
'Tenderness found in the ___'
always becomes 'Tenderness is found in <regions> with light to medium pressure palpation'

The EXAM NOTES box (posture code)

1st letter (L/R)
low hip side
2nd letter (L/R/N)
spinal translation side; N = none
3rd letter with a small number above (e.g. L15)
heavier side on the bilateral scales and the pound difference
Small A / P letters under the 3rd letter (e.g. AA, or P)
anterior/posterior HIP, then anterior/posterior SHOULDER if there is a second letter; the side is the low-hip side
Next letter (L/R)
low shoulder side
Next letter (L/R)
neck tilt side
Last letter (L/R)
head tilt side
No box at all
posture was not evaluated (nothing is written)
